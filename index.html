Credential Verification

This official page confirms whether a certificate of completion, diploma or professional credential was genuinely issued by the institute. Enter the credential number printed on the document — or simply scan the QR code on it.

Demo registry contains: BBA-2026-004812 (diploma, valid), PRC-2026-003177 (certificate, valid), FSC-2023-118246 (expired), CSA-2026-044811 (revoked). Any other number shows the “not found” state.

How this page is used

Employers, universities and licensing bodies open the link printed on the certificate (or scan its QR code) and compare the record with the paper document:

  • the name on the record must match the applicant’s identity documents;
  • the program, hours and dates must match what the CV claims;
  • the status line is the decisive element — “Valid” confirms the credential, “No credential found” means the document cannot be confirmed.
